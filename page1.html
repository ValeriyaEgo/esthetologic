Premium Laser Hair Removal

Introducing Triton: the world's first patented multi-wavelength laser hair removal system. Safe for all skin tones, from fair to dark, Triton combines Nd:YAG, Alexandrite, and Diode wavelengths to treat both deep hairs and new emerging follicles simultaneously.

BENEFITS:

* Precision: Specifically targets dark, coarse hairs without harming surrounding skin.
* Time Efficiency: Treats small areas quickly and larger areas in about an hour.
* Cost-Effective: Saves money over time compared to regular waxing or shaving.
* No Ingrown Hairs: Less likely to cause ingrown hairs.
* Smooth Skin: Leaves skin smoother compared to other methods.
* Safety: A safe procedure when performed by professionals.
* Convenience: Reduces the need for regular hair removal.
* No Hair Growth Needed: Allows for shaving between treatments.

OUR TECHNOLOGY:

* Alexandrite: Ideal for lighter skin with dark hair, using the 755 nm wavelength.
* ND: Yag: Suitable for all skin types, including darker skin, with the 1064 nm wavelength.

How Triton Works?

The painless procedure involves directing a concentrated light beam at unwanted hair, damaging the follicles and reducing their ability to grow hair. Expect to see a 10% to 25% reduction in hair after the first session.

Got Questions? We Have Answers!

Post-Treatment Care

- Avoid sun exposure and use SPF 30+ sunscreen.
- Apply a cold compress to reduce discomfort.
- Gently wash and pat the treated area dry.
- Avoid makeup, deodorants, or lotions for a day if the skin is irritated.

Booking Your Next Session

- Face: 4-6 weeks
- Bikini & Underarm: 4-8 weeks
- Legs, Back, & Chest: 4-8 weeks

Precautions

- Avoid sun exposure and don’t wax or pluck before treatment.
- Shave the area before your appointment.
- Post-treatment, keep the area protected from the sun and avoid heat.

Sessions Needed

Typically, 3-7 sessions are required for the best results.

Risks and Side Effects

Common side effects include redness, swelling, and itching. Rarely, treatment can lead to burns or more hair growth in the area

Worth It?

Yes, for long-term hair reduction, it's more cost-effective and time-saving than other methods
